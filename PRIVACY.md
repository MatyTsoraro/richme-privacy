# Privacy Policy for RichMe

Last updated: November 14, 2026
App: RichMe (com.richme.app)
Contact: maty.tsoraro@gmail.com

## Overview
RichMe is a personal savings app. You save a little every day, without a bank, without judgment.
We designed RichMe to collect as little data as possible.

## What we do NOT collect
We do NOT collect:
- Bank account numbers, bank login, or bank credentials
- Credit card numbers
- Your name, home address, or phone number
- Location
- Contacts, photos, or files
- Browsing history

RichMe does NOT connect to your bank.

## What is stored on your device
Your savings goals, amounts, dates, and app settings are stored locally on your device.
This data stays on your device unless you delete the app.
We do not receive a copy of your savings data on our servers, because we do not operate servers for RichMe.

## PRO subscription
If you buy RichMe PRO, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing.
We do not see or store your payment card details.
To check your subscription status, the app may use RevenueCat / Google Play Billing with an anonymous app user ID. This is used only to know if PRO is active. It is not used to identify you personally.

## Third-party services
RichMe uses only what is needed to run the app and to manage PRO:
- Google Play Services / Google Play Billing - for app distribution and purchases
- RevenueCat - for subscription status

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data for advertising.

## If you contact us
If you email us at maty.tsoraro@gmail.com, we will receive the email address you send from and the content of your message, only to reply to you. We will not use it for marketing.

## Data deletion
Because your savings data is stored locally, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
If you contacted us by email and want that email deleted, write to maty.tsoraro@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## Children
RichMe is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the Last updated date above.

## Contact
For any privacy question: maty.tsoraro@gmail.com

## מה אנחנו לא אוספים
אנחנו לא אוספים:
- מספרי חשבון בנק, סיסמאות לבנק או פרטי כניסה לבנק
